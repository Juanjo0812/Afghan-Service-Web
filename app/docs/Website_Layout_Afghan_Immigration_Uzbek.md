## 3. HUQUQLARINGIZNI BILING (KNOW YOUR RIGHTS)

### Sarlavha (Title)

**Huquqlaringizni biling (Know Your Rights)**

### 1-bo'lim: Politsiya yoki immigratsiya xodimlari bilan muloqotda (Section 1: When interacting with police or immigration agents)

- Immigratsiya maqomingiz haqidagi savollarga javob berishga majbur emassiz (You do not have to answer questions about your immigration status)
- Siz quyidagilarni aytishingiz mumkin: "Men sukut saqlash huquqimdan foydalanmoqchiman" (You may say: "I want to remain silent")
- Ruxsatnomasiz o'tkaziladigan tintuvni rad etish huquqiga egasiz (You have the right to refuse a search without a warrant)
- Nimadir imzolashdan avval advokat talab qiling (Ask for a lawyer before signing anything)

### 2-bo'lim: Agar immigratsiya xizmati (ICE) uyingizga kelsa (Section 2: If ICE comes to your home)

- Imzolangan ruxsatnomasiz eshikni **OCHMANG** (Do NOT open the door without a signed warrant)
- Uni deraza orqali yoki eshik tagidan ko'rsatishni so'rang (Ask to see it through the window/under the door)
- Siz sukut saqlash huquqiga egasiz (You have the right to remain silent)
- Oilaviy xavfsizlik rejasini tuzing (Have a family safety plan)

### 3-bo'lim: Hujjatlarni xavfsiz saqlash va olib yurish (Section 3: Carrying documents safely)

- Hujjatlarning aslini emas, nusxasini olib yuring (Carry copies, not originals)
- Zarurat bo'lmasa, pasportni o'zingiz bilan olib yurmang (Avoid carrying passport unless advised)
- Hujjatlarni uyda xavfsiz joyda saqlang (Store documents safely at home)
