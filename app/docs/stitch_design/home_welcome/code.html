Our Mission

Building community and providing essential legal support.

The Afghan Support Network provides comprehensive, culturally competent services to Afghan families. We combine expert legal advocacy with deep community roots to ensure every family has the tools they need to thrive in their new home.

Our services are free, completely confidential, and provided by professionals who understand both the legal landscape and the cultural context.
