Free Immigration Assistance for Afghan Families in Phoenix

Professional, confidential, and culturally competent legal services to help you navigate your immigration journey in the United States. Our team is dedicated to providing clear guidance and support every step of the way.

translate Available in: English, Pashto, Dari, Uzbek

Our Services

description

Asylum Applications

Complete assistance with filing form I-589, preparing personal declarations, and gathering supporting evidence for affirmative asylum cases.

work

Work Permit Assistance

Guidance on applying for initial Employment Authorization Documents (EAD) and processing renewals to ensure uninterrupted work eligibility.

shield

TPS (Temporary Protected Status)

Support in registering for or renewing Temporary Protected Status for eligible Afghan nationals residing in the US.

family_home

Green Card & Family Reunification

Help with adjustment of status (I-485) and filing petitions to bring family members to the United States safely.

gavel

Afghan Adjustment Act Assistance

Dedicated resources and legal preparation for pathways to permanent residency specifically designed for Afghan evacuees and humanitarian parolees.
