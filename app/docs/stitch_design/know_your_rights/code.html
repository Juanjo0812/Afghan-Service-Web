Know Your Rights

Regardless of your immigration status, you have fundamental rights in the United States. Learn how to protect yourself and your family when interacting with law enforcement or immigration officials.

  • You have the right to remain silent. You do not have to answer questions about where you were born, whether you are a U.S. citizen, or how you entered the country.
  • Do not lie or show false documents.
  • You do not have to consent to a search of yourself or your belongings, but an officer may "pat down" your clothes if they suspect you have a weapon.
  • Ask if you are free to leave. If the officer says yes, walk away calmly.
