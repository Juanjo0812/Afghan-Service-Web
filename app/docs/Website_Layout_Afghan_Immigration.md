## 3. Know Your Rights

### Title

**Know Your Rights**

### Section 1: When Interacting with Police or Immigration Agents

- You do not have to answer questions about your immigration status
- You may say: "I want to remain silent"
- You have the right to refuse a search without a warrant
- Ask for a lawyer before signing anything

### Section 2: If ICE Comes to Your Home

- Do **NOT** open the door without a signed warrant
- Ask to see it through the window / under the door
- You have the right to remain silent
- Have a family safety plan

### Section 3: Carrying Documents Safely

- Carry copies, not originals
- Avoid carrying passport unless advised
- Store documents safely at home
